SOL / TAPSTITCH TOOL
Order assistant.
01 / Extract Tapstitch product

Open a Tapstitch product details page, then extract its name, item number, sizes, colors and loaded product images.

Ready when you're on a Tapstitch product page.
02 / Fill customer address

Copy order data JSON from SOL Studio, open Tapstitch checkout → Edit address, paste it here, then fill the fields.

This extension only acts when you press its buttons. It never saves the Tapstitch address, clicks Save, chooses a shipping service, pays, or submits an order. Review all extracted details and filled fields manually.
