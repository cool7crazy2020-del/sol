Returns & Refunds

We want you to feel good about your SOL order. This policy explains when a return may be requested, how returned items are reviewed, and what happens if an order arrives damaged or incorrect.

Effective November 24, 2026

Quick overview: Eligible unworn items may be requested for a size or fit return within 14 calendar days of delivery. Returns must be approved by SOL before being mailed back. Items are inspected before a refund is issued.

1. Return window & eligibility

Contact SOL within 14 calendar days after delivery to request a return. A return is not automatic; SOL must approve the request and provide return instructions before the item is shipped.

For size or fit returns, items must be:

  • Unworn, unwashed, and unused.
  • Free from stains, odors, damage, alterations, and signs of wear.
  • Returned with original tags and packaging where provided.
  • The same item, size, and color supplied for the original order.

Items that do not meet these conditions may be declined or returned to the customer. Items made with customer-provided personalization or special custom details may be final sale unless they arrive defective or incorrect, or applicable law requires otherwise.

2. Size & fit returns

SOL may accept eligible size or fit returns even though products are made to order. Because each item is produced for an order, a fit return is not guaranteed until SOL reviews and approves the request.

If approved, the customer is responsible for the cost of shipping the item back to SOL. Original shipping charges are generally non-refundable for a size or fit return. If the return is accepted after inspection, SOL will refund the eligible item price to the original payment method.

Exchanges are subject to availability and are not guaranteed. If the requested replacement is unavailable, SOL may offer a refund for the eligible returned item instead.

3. How to request a return

  1. Contact SOL within 14 calendar days of delivery through our official TikTok account.
  2. Include your order number, the item you want to return, the reason, and clear photos if there is a visible issue.
  3. Wait for written approval and return instructions. Do not send anything before SOL confirms the return address.
  4. If approved, ship the item within 7 calendar days using a trackable shipping service and send SOL the tracking number.
  5. Keep your shipping receipt until the return is completed.

Customers are responsible for items until they are delivered to the approved return address. SOL is not responsible for packages lost in return transit.

4. Inspection & refunds

When a return arrives, SOL will inspect it for condition and eligibility. We will notify the customer whether the return is accepted. If approved, refunds are submitted to the original payment method, generally within 5–10 business days after approval. The time it takes for the refund to appear depends on the bank or payment provider.

If an item fails inspection, SOL will explain why the return was declined. The item may be shipped back to the customer, with return shipping arranged at the customer's expense.

5. Damaged, defective, or incorrect items

If your order arrives damaged, defective, misprinted, or different from what you ordered, contact SOL within 7 calendar days of delivery. Include your order number and clear photos of the item, packaging, and issue. Please keep the item until SOL responds.

If the issue is confirmed to be our responsibility, SOL will work with you on an appropriate solution, which may include a replacement or refund. SOL will cover reasonable return or replacement shipping for a confirmed fulfillment or product defect. Do not return the item unless SOL provides instructions.

6. Cancellations & order changes

Orders may begin production shortly after they are placed. Contact SOL as soon as possible if you need to request a cancellation or change. We cannot guarantee changes or cancellations once an order has entered production, been fulfilled, or shipped. If a cancellation is approved before production begins, the eligible payment will be refunded to the original payment method.

7. Returned items & resale

Items accepted back for size or fit reasons may be added to SOL inventory only after inspection. A returned item will only be offered to another customer if it is genuinely in like-new condition, clean, unworn, unwashed, undamaged, and complete with its original tags and packaging where provided. Any item that does not meet this standard will not be resold as new.

8. Non-returnable situations

Unless an item is defective, incorrect, or applicable law says otherwise, SOL may decline returns for items that have been worn, washed, altered, damaged after delivery, or returned without approval. We may also decline requests made after the return window or items missing required tags or components.

9. Contact SOL

To start a return or report an order issue, message SOL through our official TikTok account. Include your order number and the email used at checkout. Never post personal order details publicly.

10. Your legal rights

This policy does not limit any consumer rights that cannot legally be excluded. If this policy conflicts with applicable consumer protection law, the law will apply.
