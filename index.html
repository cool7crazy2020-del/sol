SOL Tapstitch extractor

Open any Tapstitch product page, then use the SOL Extractor side panel. No product-link pasting needed.

Download latest SOL Extractor · v1.1.0View extension files
  1. Download and unzip the extension.
  2. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and select the sol-extractor folder inside the unzipped SOL folder.
  4. Reload your Tapstitch product tab once so Chrome loads the extractor, then open SOL.
  5. Click the SOL Extractor puzzle-piece/extension icon in Chrome and pin it if you want.
  6. Open a signed-in Tapstitch product page, open the SOL Extractor side panel, and click Extract product.
  7. Click Send to SOL Studio. The product name, mockups, sizes, and colors are sent straight to the Studio form.

The extension only reads the active Tapstitch product page when you press Extract. Only loaded images can be collected; temporary or protected image URLs may not work in the public store.
